3. What On Ice is — and is not

On Ice is a self-directed tool that shields shopping apps and websites you choose, offers a short pause, lets you note down what caught your eye, holds it for a period you set, and records on your device what you decided. It also offers a journal, things to do instead, goals, limits and blocks. It is designed to turn an automatic purchase into a decision you actually make.

On Ice is not a medical, psychological or therapeutic service, and nothing in it — including feelings you tap, pauses, reminders, journal questions, insights or AI-written text — is advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are struggling with compulsive spending, debt, anxiety or anything else that affects your wellbeing, please speak to a qualified professional or a debt-advice service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

On Ice is also not a security or parental-control product. It is designed to be exactly as strong as you ask it to be: the app can be deleted, Screen Time access can be withdrawn in iOS Settings, and shields exist only while the app is installed and authorised.

5. Screen Time, blocks and strict mode

On Ice relies on Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple enforces the shield; On Ice asks for it. Whether a shield appears, how quickly, whether a usage step is reported, and whether shields persist across restarts and iOS updates is ultimately determined by iOS, and we cannot guarantee it in every circumstance. On Ice cannot prevent a purchase — it can only put a screen in front of a shop you chose.

Some tools you can switch on offer no way through while they run: a hard-block rule during its hours, a daily limit once it is reached (until your next day begins), a timed block until it ends, and a Focus block while that Focus is on. A timed block set to Only these apps shields every app on your device except the essential apps you chose. Before starting one, make sure you can reach what you might need — for calls, messages, maps, payments, health or work.

Strict mode is a commitment you make to yourself. While it runs, for the time you chose, protection stays on, rules stay as they are, shops can be added but not removed, and limits and your day start cannot be changed — including by you, and including by us. We cannot end strict mode, a block or a rule early on request. If you set up Someone holds the key, the person holding the PIN can end strict mode early, and you can request a delayed escape that ends it 24 hours later; we cannot recover a forgotten PIN. Do not start a commitment you are not prepared to keep, and do not start one on a device you may need unrestricted access to.

Deleting the app ends every shield, block and limit, as described in section 3.

7. Premium, subscriptions and billing

On Ice is free to download and use. On Ice Premium unlocks additional features and is sold in five forms, at the price shown in the app and on the App Store for your country:

  • Premium Monthly — an auto-renewing subscription, billed monthly.
  • Premium Yearly — an auto-renewing subscription, billed yearly, beginning with a free trial where one is offered.
  • Premium Lifetime — a one-time purchase that does not renew.
  • Premium Family Yearly — an auto-renewing subscription, billed yearly, beginning with a free trial where one is offered, for up to six people through Family Sharing.
  • Premium Family Lifetime — a one-time purchase that does not renew, for up to six people through Family Sharing.

The two family plans are shared through Apple's Family Sharing with up to five other members of your family group. Each person gets Premium in their own copy of On Ice, on their own device; no shops, lists, journals, savings or settings are shared between them. Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime are for you alone and are not shared.

Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. A free trial converts to a paid subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically at the same price and period unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; renewal is charged within the 24 hours before that period ends. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store subscription settings; cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period, and no partial refund is given for the unused part of a period. Buying a lifetime plan, or receiving a family plan, does not cancel a subscription you already have — cancel it yourself. Refunds are handled by Apple under Apple's policies. Use Restore purchases in the app to recover Premium on a new device.

If a subscription lapses, nothing you have already built is removed: your list, journal, history and estimated savings stay, the shops you chose stay shielded, and existing rules, limits and a running strict mode keep holding. Your wait returns to 24 hours, the AI reflection switches off, and widgets and pattern screens wait for Premium to return. Only the ability to add more Premium features closes. The set of Premium features may change over time; we will not remove a feature from an active subscription without notice.

8. The AI reflection

The optional AI reflection is off until you switch it on. With Apple Intelligence as its writer, text is generated on your device under Apple's terms. With OpenAI as its writer, the details listed in the Privacy Policy are sent to our service and on to OpenAI so that a short response can be generated. Earlier versions of the app use Google's Gemini model on Vertex AI for this instead. The journal's Reflect on my week uses Apple Intelligence on your device only.

What a language model writes is generated text. It may be wrong, generic or unhelpful, it is not advice of any kind, and it is never a statement of fact about your money. Do not type anything into that screen you would not want processed by a third-party model. If a request fails, the app simply shows you the ordinary choices — nothing is queued, retried later or stored. We may limit how many reflections a device can request each day.
