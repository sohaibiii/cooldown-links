3. What Cooldown is — and is not

Cooldown is a self-directed tool that shields shopping apps and websites you choose, lets you note down what caught your eye, holds it for a period you set, and records on your phone what you decided. It is designed to turn an automatic purchase into a decision you actually make.

Cooldown is not a medical, psychological or therapeutic service, and nothing in it — including feelings you tap, follow-up questions, insights or the optional reflection — is advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you are struggling with compulsive spending, debt, anxiety or anything else that affects your wellbeing, please speak to a qualified professional or a debt-advice service. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Cooldown is also not a security or parental-control product. It is designed to be exactly as strong as you ask it to be: the app can be deleted, Screen Time access can be withdrawn in iOS Settings, and the shield exists only while the app is installed and authorised.

4. Estimates, not financial advice

Every price in Cooldown is a figure you enter. The app does not connect to any bank, payment provider or retailer, does not read your transactions or order history, does not track prices, and does not verify what anything costs.

The savings figure Cooldown shows is therefore an estimate assembled from your own guesses, and is labelled as such everywhere it appears. It is not a statement of fact about money you have, a financial record, or anything you should rely on for budgeting, tax, credit or any other decision. TOPSOL is not a financial adviser, and nothing in the app or on this site is financial, investment or debt advice.

Cooldown carries no affiliate links and earns nothing from anything you buy or do not buy. Its only revenue is Premium, paid by you through Apple.

5. Screen Time, strict mode and hard blocks

Cooldown relies on Apple's Screen Time framework. Apple enforces the shield; Cooldown asks for it. Whether a shield appears, how quickly, and whether it persists across restarts and iOS updates is ultimately determined by iOS, and we cannot guarantee it in every circumstance. Cooldown cannot prevent a purchase — it can only put a screen in front of a shop you chose, which you remain free to pass.

Strict mode is a commitment you make to yourself. While it runs, for the duration you chose, your setup cannot be undone — including by you, and including by us. We cannot end a strict-mode session early on request. Do not start one you are not prepared to keep, and do not start one on a phone you may need unrestricted access to.

A hard-block rule offers no way through during its hours. The same warning applies. Deleting the app ends every shield, as described in section 3.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. A free trial converts to a paid subscription unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends. 8. The AI reflection

The optional AI reflection is off until you switch it on. When it is on, the text listed in the Privacy Policy is sent to our service and on to Google's Gemini model on Vertex AI so that a short response can be generated.

What a language model writes is generated text. It may be wrong, generic or unhelpful, it is not advice of any kind, and it is never a statement of fact about your money. Do not enter anything into that screen you would not want processed by a third-party model. If a request fails, the app simply shows you the ordinary choice screen — nothing is queued, retried or stored for later.
