How to stop late night online shopping

The order you wonder about in the morning was usually placed late, in bed, with one thumb. There is no mystery in it. You were tired, the phone was there, and the shop never closes. So close it yourself.

By the On Ice team · Updated 28 November 2026 · 5 min read

Why night is different

Three things line up after dark. You are tired, and a tired mind takes the easy option, which a shop has made sure is "buy". You are alone, with nobody to ask whether you need it. And the phone is the last thing in your hand before sleep.

Meanwhile the shop is wide awake. Offers often end at midnight, and a lot of marketing email lands in the evening. You are not weak at night. Night is simply when selling is easiest.

That is good news, because a problem with a fixed hour is much easier to fix than a problem with you.

Close the shops on a schedule with Downtime

Downtime is part of Screen Time on every iPhone. Between the hours you set, only the apps you allow stay open.

  1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time, then Downtime.
  2. Turn on Scheduled and set the hours, for example from 10 p.m. to 7 a.m. You can choose different hours for different days.
  3. Go back one screen and tap Always Allowed. Keep what you need at night, such as Phone, Messages, Clock and a reading or music app. Leave shopping apps off the list.

During those hours the other app icons are dimmed. Safari is an app like any other, so if you leave it off the allowed list the shop websites are covered too. If you want Safari at night, block the shop sites separately: see how to block shopping websites on iPhone.

Where it stops working. Tap a dimmed app and iOS offers a way round: a button that leads to Ignore Limit For Today. To remove it you need a Screen Time passcode with Block at Downtime switched on, and that only holds if someone else keeps the passcode. Downtime is also blunt. It closes everything you forgot to allow, and the first night that gets in your way is usually the night it gets switched off.

Make the evening phone quieter

  • Turn off shop notifications. Settings, Notifications, each shopping app, Allow Notifications off. This matters most in the evening, when the "ends tonight" messages are sent.
  • Use a Sleep Focus. In Settings, tap Focus, then Sleep, and give it a schedule. It doesn't block any app, but it holds notifications back until morning. A deal alert you read at 8 a.m. is a lot less persuasive than one you read at 11 p.m.
  • Sign out of the shops you overuse. Having to find a password at midnight is a surprisingly good block.

Move the phone, not only the apps

The simplest fix on this page costs nothing. Charge the phone somewhere you can't reach from bed: across the room, or outside the bedroom. If the phone is your alarm, a basic alarm clock replaces it. Put a book where the phone used to be.

You can still get up and fetch it. Almost nobody does, for a pair of trainers.

Let the want wait until morning

If something does catch your eye late, don't argue with it. Write it down: the item and the price. Then close the tab. Don't leave it in the basket, because the shop will email you about it before breakfast.

In the morning, read the note. If you still want it in daylight, buy it. That is the 24 hour rule, and night is where it earns its keep.
