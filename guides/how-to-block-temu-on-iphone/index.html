How to block Temu on iPhone (the app, the website and the alerts)

Blocking Temu on an iPhone means covering four things, not one: the app, the website, the notifications and the emails. Miss one and the shop is still a tap away. Here they are in order.

By the On Ice team · Updated 28 November 2026 · 6 min read

Like most shopping apps, Temu gives you plenty of reasons to open it: coupons with a timer, daily deals, delivery updates. That is why deleting the app on its own rarely lasts. The website still opens in Safari, and the messages keep arriving. Work through the four steps below and each of those doors is closed.

1. Block the Temu app

You have two built in choices. A daily limit keeps the app but covers it once your time is used. Deleting it and blocking installs removes it properly.

A daily limit

  1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time. If it has never been set up, turn on App & Website Activity first.
  2. Tap App Limits, then Add Limit.
  3. Open the Shopping & Food category and tick Temu.
  4. Tap Next, set the time (the smallest is 1 minute a day) and tap Add.

Where it stops working. When the time runs out, iOS covers the app, and under the cover is a button that leads to Ignore Limit For Today. One tap and the limit is gone until midnight. You can close that door with a Screen Time passcode (Screen Time, then Lock Screen Time Settings) and Block at End of Limit switched on for the limit, but it only holds if someone else knows the passcode and you don't.

Delete it and stop it coming back

  1. Press and hold the Temu icon, tap Remove App, then Delete App.
  2. In Settings, go to Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, and turn the switch on.
  3. Tap iTunes & App Store Purchases, then Installing Apps, and choose Don't Allow.

The catch is that the App Store disappears from your Home Screen while this is on, so you can't install or update anything else either.

Deleting the app doesn't close your account. Your Temu account, saved card and order history stay where they are. If you want those gone too, close the account from the account settings inside Temu before you delete the app.

2. Block temu.com in Safari

This is the step most people skip, and it is the one that matters. Without it, typing three letters into Safari puts you back in the shop.

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Content & Privacy Restrictions, and turn the switch on.
  2. Tap Content Restrictions. On newer versions of iOS this row is called App Store, Media, Web & Games.
  3. Tap Web Content and choose Limit Adult Websites.
  4. Under Never Allow, tap Add Website and enter temu.com.

Open temu.com now and Safari shows a page saying the site is restricted. The same goes for other browsers on the phone. If your Temu opens on a different address, add the one you see in the address bar.

Two side effects. Limit Adult Websites also switches on Apple's adult content filter, and it removes Private Browsing from Safari. We go through the alternatives in how to block shopping websites on iPhone.

3. Turn off the notifications

If you are keeping the app, this is the quickest win on the page. Open Settings, tap Notifications, choose Temu and switch off Allow Notifications. A coupon that expires tonight is an invitation, and you can't accept one you never saw.

4. Stop the emails and texts

You don't need a clear out. The next time a Temu email arrives, scroll to the bottom and unsubscribe from that one. For marketing texts, replying STOP ends them in the US and many other countries. A few days of this and the inbox goes quiet.

What a block can't do: the adverts

A lot of people searching for this are really asking about Temu's adverts on other websites, in games and on social apps. It is worth being straight about that: blocking temu.com stops you opening the shop, but it does not remove the adverts. They are placed by advertising networks, and no iPhone setting hides one advertiser.

Two things turn them down a little:

  • In Settings, open Privacy & Security, then Tracking, and switch off Allow Apps to Request to Track. It makes it harder for adverts to follow you from one app to the next.
  • On most social apps you can open the menu on an advert and choose to hide adverts from that advertiser.

The useful part is what happens when you tap one anyway. With steps 1 and 2 done, the advert lands on a blocked page, not a checkout.
