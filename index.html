Questions people ask first

Will On Ice lock me out of my shopping apps?

No. An ordinary shield is a pause, not a wall. Tap Note it, say what caught your eye, and I need to shop now opens the shop for 1, 5 or 15 minutes. The only things with no way through are ones you set up yourself, such as a daily limit you have reached, a timed block or a hard rule for one shop. Each of those ends on its own.

Does it block shopping websites, or only apps?

Both. Add a shop's app and its website separately and each is shielded, the website in Safari.

Does it work with Amazon, Temu, Shein and the rest?

You pick the apps in Apple's own Screen Time picker and add websites by address, so it works with whichever shops get you. On Ice never learns the names of the apps you pick. Apple hands it private codes instead.

Does On Ice see what I buy?

No. It has no access to your bank, your orders, your browsing or your card. It records only its own events: that a shield was shown, and what you chose. Those stay on your device.

What happens to the thing I noted?

It waits on your list for 24 hours. Then On Ice asks once: still want it? If you let it go, the price you guessed joins your estimated savings. If you still want it, the shop opens for you. Either answer is a good one.

Is On Ice free?

Yes, and free is a working product, not a demo: one shopping app and one website shielded, the 24 hour wait and your list, your estimated savings, the breathing pause, three things to do instead, the journal, a daily shopping time goal, and timed blocks.

Which devices does it need?

An iPhone or iPad running iOS 17 or newer. There is no account. Each device keeps its own shops, list and history, and nothing syncs between them.
