What is stored where

On Ice has no account and keeps no profile of anyone on a server. Everything it holds is on your device, and you can remove it without asking us.

An account, a profile, anything with your name on it—There is none. We never had one to delete.

Remove it in the app

  • Journal entries: open an entry and tap Delete entry.
  • Spend log: tap Remove on an entry.
  • Rules, limits, reminders and activities: each has its own delete or remove button.
  • Shops: change your selection, or switch a shop off on the home screen. Turning protection off takes every shield down at once.
  • Decided items stay in your history — that is what your estimated savings and patterns are made of. To remove that history, delete the app.
While strict mode is running, your setup is held in place — shops can't be removed, and rules and limits can't be deleted. That is the one commitment you asked the app to keep for you. It ends on its own at the time you chose.

Your journal

Journal entries live only on your device, and On Ice never sent them anywhere, so there is nothing to remove elsewhere. Delete them one at a time with Delete entry. To keep a copy before you delete the app, use Export journal: it makes a plain-text file that you save wherever you choose.

The AI reflection

If you switched on the AI reflection, On Ice keeps the latest weekly write-up on your device so you can read it again. Switching the AI reflection off in Settings → AI reflection deletes it, removes the Sunday reminder, ends the app's session with the service, and returns On Ice to making no network calls of its own.

The service never held a copy of what you sent. It keeps only a per-device count under a random key, which it deletes after two days, and daily totals that name no device.

Delete the app

Deleting On Ice from your iPhone or iPad removes all of its data with it — your list, history, journal, settings and Screen Time tokens — and ends every shield, block and limit immediately. It is the way to erase everything at once. If the app is included in an iCloud or computer backup, that copy belongs to your Apple Account and is governed by Apple's terms; we have no access to it.

Your subscription

Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription — it belongs to your Apple Account, not to the app. Cancel it in App Store subscription settings; it stays active until the end of the current period. Refunds are handled by Apple Support. A lifetime purchase does not renew and needs nothing cancelled. If you belong to a family plan, the purchaser manages it.

Ask us

If you would like written confirmation that we hold nothing about you, or you believe we do, write to topsol.org@gmail.com from any address. We will reply within 30 days. Please do not send us your list, your prices, your journal or screenshots of them — we do not need them to answer.
