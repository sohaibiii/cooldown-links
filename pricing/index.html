The small print, in plain words.

What happens if my subscription ends?

Nothing you built is taken away. Your list, history and estimated savings stay, the shops you chose stay shielded, and your rules, limits and a running strict mode keep holding. Your wait goes back to 24 hours, the AI reflection switches off, and widgets and the pattern screens wait for Premium to return.

How do the family plans work?

Family Yearly and Family Lifetime are shared through Apple's Family Sharing with up to five other people in your family group, six in all. Each person gets Premium in their own copy of On Ice, on their own device. Nobody sees anyone else's shops, list, journal or savings.

Can I restore Premium on a new phone?

Yes. Open Settings in On Ice and tap Restore purchases. Premium belongs to your Apple Account, not to the device. Your list and journal live on the device that made them and move only with a backup of the whole device.

Does On Ice make money from what I buy?

No. There are no affiliate links, no ads and no price tracking. Premium is the only way On Ice earns anything, which is why it has no reason to nudge you toward a checkout.
